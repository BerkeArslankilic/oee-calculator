# OEE Calculator

I spent a lot of time on the theoretical side for this one. It takes your raw production data and calculates your availability, performance, and quality metrics to give you a clear interpreted OEE score.

## How it works
Just input your shift length, planned/unplanned downtime, ideal cycle time, and production counts. The tool instantly calculates the core manufacturing metrics:
- **Availability**
- **Performance**
- **Quality**
- **Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE)**

It also gives you a visual interpretation (World Class, Typical, or Needs Improvement) and a detailed mathematical breakdown of how the numbers were calculated. Runs entirely in your browser with a sleek dark theme.
